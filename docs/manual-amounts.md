# Spools without a reading

[← README](../README.md) · [Using the guard](using.md) · [The running print](running-print.md) · [Learning](learning.md) · **Spools without a reading** · [Limits](limits.md)

A spool without an RFID tag, or on an external holder, reports no amount. Enter it on the card ("Amount in …"): put the spool on a scale and type what the scale shows and what the empty spool weighs. The filament is the difference, shown as you type. The window starts with the empty spool's weight you entered last (`empty_spool_g`; 250 g at first, about a Bambu spool); enter 0 there when you weighed the filament alone or already took the spool off. The result then says that nothing is taken off for the spool, and 0 is not kept: the next window starts with the last weight above 0. Save stores the amount; if it cannot be stored just then, the window stays open and says so. The guard then counts down, erring to the low side:

- A print is taken off in full when it starts to run, also when it is cancelled later.
- What a print needs beyond the amount of its tray comes off the other loaded spools of the same filament (the same material and colour, the same product where both name one, for the same nozzle), the fullest first: with AMS auto refill the print goes on from one of them. An entered spool has its part taken off its amount when the print is counted; a spool the printer measures shows it in its reading. When the print's own tray has no amount (no tag and nothing entered, or an amount out of date), how much goes beyond it cannot be told, and the amounts entered for that filament are out of date.
- An amount entered while a print runs is what the spool held then: what that print still needs of it comes off at the guard's next look at the printer. When how far the print is cannot be told (it was sliced on this computer, but its G-code did not add up layer by layer), all it needs comes off. A print the guard cannot count at all (see the next point) makes the amount out of date at that look, as if it had started after the amount was entered: enter it again when that print is over.
- A print the guard cannot count (not sliced on this computer, or its tray cannot be told) makes the amount "out of date": unknown again, until it is entered again. So does a print of a filament that Bambu Studio gives no weight (a profile with density 0), for the spool that filament prints from.
- When the printer was not followed for ten minutes or more (the computer asleep, the link to the printer down) and shows another print than the last one counted, every amount entered for it is out of date. The printer counts as followed only while it reports: after a minute without a report the guard ends its connection and makes a new one, looking the printer's address up again.
- An entered amount ends when the printer reports another material or colour in that tray, starts to measure the tray itself (a spool with a tag), or reports the tray empty: the spool was taken out. The guard sees that at a check and, while it follows the printer, at any time, once two looks in a row agree; the log names the tray and the reason. An amount typed while the spool is still on the scale stands until the printer has seen the spool back in its tray, and ends when the tray is empty after that. A status that shows no spool in any AMS ends nothing: it may be a report still in pieces.
- The external holder never reports empty. After changing to another spool of the same material and colour there, enter the amount again: nothing tells the guard that the spool is another one. The same holds for an AMS tray whose spool is changed while the printer is off.

To count, the guard keeps a standing read-only connection to a printer for as long as it has an entered amount (and while learning has a reason to: see [Learning](learning.md#what-is-recorded)). Nothing is counted while the guard is not running: an amount entered then (on the card of `check --popup`) is out of date when the guard first looks at the printer ten minutes or more after it was entered, because the printer may have printed in between. A printer in LAN-only mode names every job alike, so a print sent again looks like the one before it. The guard keeps whether it saw the last print it counted end: found on the printer again after that, it is another print, taken off like any other, or making the amounts out of date when the printer was not followed for ten minutes or more in between. While the printer prepares a print, the check takes it off the entered amount, whatever was counted before. Not seen: a print started and finished while the printer was not followed, when the printer names it exactly like the one before; nor a print that took the place of one the guard last saw running. The amount window of a card without a running guard says so: nothing counts the amount down until one runs.
