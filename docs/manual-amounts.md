# Spools without a reading

[← README](../README.md) · [Using the guard](using.md) · [The running print](running-print.md) · [Learning](learning.md) · **Spools without a reading** · [Limits](limits.md)

A spool without an RFID tag, or on an external holder, reports no amount. Enter it on the card ("Amount in …"): put the spool on a scale and type what the scale shows and what the empty spool weighs. The filament is the difference, shown as you type. The window starts with the empty spool's weight you entered last (`empty_spool_g`; 250 g at first, about a Bambu spool); enter 0 there when you weighed the filament alone or already took the spool off. The guard then counts down, erring to the low side:

- A print is taken off in full when it starts to run, also when it is cancelled later.
- What a print needs beyond the amount of its tray comes off the other entered spools of the same filament that are loaded (the same material and colour, the same product where both name one, for the same nozzle), the fullest first: with AMS auto refill the print goes on from one of them. When the print's own tray has no amount (no tag and nothing entered, or an amount out of date), how much goes beyond it cannot be told, and the amounts entered for that filament are out of date.
- An amount entered while a print runs is what the spool held then: what that print still needs of it comes off at the guard's next look at the printer. When how far the print is cannot be told (it was sliced on this computer, but its G-code did not add up layer by layer), all it needs comes off. A print the guard cannot count at all (see the next point) makes the amount out of date at that look, as if it had started after the amount was entered: enter it again when that print is over.
- A print the guard cannot count (not sliced on this computer, or its tray cannot be told) makes the amount "out of date": unknown again, until it is entered again.
- When the printer was not followed for ten minutes or more (the computer asleep, the link to the printer down) and shows another print than the last one counted, every amount entered for it is out of date. The printer counts as followed only while it reports: after a minute without a report the guard ends its connection and makes a new one, looking the printer's address up again.
- The entry ends when the printer reports another material or colour in that tray.

To count, the guard keeps a standing read-only connection to a printer for as long as it has an entered amount (and while learning has a reason to: see [Learning](learning.md#what-is-recorded)). A printer in LAN-only mode names every job alike, so a print sent again looks like the one before it. The guard keeps whether it saw the last print it counted end: found on the printer again after that, it is another print, taken off like any other, or making the amounts out of date when the printer was not followed for ten minutes or more in between. While the printer prepares a print, the check takes it off the entered amount, whatever was counted before. Not seen: a print started and finished while the printer was not followed, when the printer names it exactly like the one before; nor a print that took the place of one the guard last saw running.
